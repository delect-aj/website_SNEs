Ecological dysbiosis score

One number, placed against 10,276 reference samples. It says how unusual a community is in the direction this model associates with disease — not what disease, not how likely, and not whether anyone is ill.

What this score is not

Read this before using the number

The model is trained on human faecal 16S data only. It has no meaning for soil, water, oral, skin or vaginal communities, and it has no meaning for a sample that is not 16S.

The reference cohort spans thirteen diseases. The model was trained across them, so its accuracy on a disease it has never seen is lower than its accuracy on this cohort — AUC 0.64 by leave-one-disease-out, against 0.80 within the cohort. A community from a condition outside the thirteen will therefore sit closer to the middle than it should.

It is one number from one model on one sample. Gut communities vary far more within a person over a month than the difference this score is trying to detect, so a single sample cannot support a conclusion about an individual.

Research use only. Not a medical device; not for diagnosis, treatment or prevention of any disease. No sample data is stored: uploads are processed in memory and discarded.
