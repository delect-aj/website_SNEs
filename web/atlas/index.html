Interactive atlas

Gut Microbial Social Niche Atlas

Each point represents one OTU, positioned according to its ecological embedding. OTUs that lie close together have similar co-occurrence patterns and therefore occupy similar ecological niches, independent of their phylogenetic relatedness. Select an OTU to compare its ecological and phylogenetic neighbours side by side.

Methods and interpretation

Layout. Point positions are a UMAP projection of the 100-dimensional ecological embeddings. Distances in two dimensions are approximate and should not be interpreted quantitatively; the neighbour lists give cosine similarities in the full embedding space.

Trait prediction. Traits are predicted by random forest classifiers trained on SNE vectors, with Traitar genome annotations as labels. Performance is estimated by leave-one-phylum-out cross-validation, in which all members of the test phylum are excluded from training. This is stricter than random splitting, and the resulting AUC is shown next to every prediction.

Sequence search. Pasted or uploaded sequences are aligned on the server against the representative sequences of all 14,093 OTUs at 97% identity, and are deleted after the search. A short amplicon may be equally similar to several OTUs; in that case, all matching OTUs are listed.

Hidden traits. Predictions for traits with a cross-validated AUC below 0.65 are hidden by default because their predictive performance is low; they can be shown at the bottom of each OTU panel. BacDive measurements and Traitar genome annotations are always shown.
