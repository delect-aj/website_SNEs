About this map, and what the numbers on a card mean

Positions come from a UMAP of the 100-dimensional ecological embedding, computed once and shipped as a binary. Distances on screen are a two-dimensional approximation and should not be read quantitatively; use the neighbour lists on a card for that.

Trait values are the output of a random forest fitted on SNE vectors, trained on Traitar genome labels. Their accuracy is estimated by holding out a whole phylum at a time: the model never sees any member of the phylum it is scored on. That is a harder test than a random split, and it is the number shown beside every value.

Traits whose held-out AUC falls below 0.65 are collapsed by default. They are still there — the fold-out at the bottom of a card has them — but they are not presented as if they were knowledge.

Research use only. Not a medical device; not for diagnosis, treatment or prevention of any disease. Trait values are statistical inferences from co-occurrence, not experimental measurements.
