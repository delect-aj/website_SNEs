Ecological embeddings of the human gut microbiome

Every one of 14,093 SILVA 97% OTUs is placed in a 100-dimensional space by the company it keeps, learned from co-occurrence across roughly 210,000 human faecal samples. Two OTUs sit close together if they tend to appear in the same communities — not because they are related.

Atlas

Browse the 14,093 OTUs as a map. Look up any taxon and see its ecological neighbours beside its phylogenetic ones — the two lists overlap less than you would expect.

Uncultured OTUs outnumber cultured ones twelve to one here, and most of them have no genome. Their trait values on the card are inferred, each carrying the cross-validated AUC it earned.

What this is, and what it is not

These embeddings describe realized niches: the role a taxon plays inside the gut community, inferred from who it co-occurs with. That is not the same as its physiology in pure culture. An OTU whose inferred trait is "anaerobic" is one that behaves like an anaerobe in the gut, partly because it keeps anaerobic company.

Every model here was trained on human faecal 16S data. Nothing on this site applies to soil, marine, oral or skin communities, and nothing on it is a diagnostic.

Research use only. Not a medical device; not for diagnosis, treatment or prevention of any disease. Predictions are statistical inferences from co-occurrence, not experimental measurements.
