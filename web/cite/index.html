Before you cite a number from this site

Trait values on the atlas are inferences, not measurements, and each one is shown with the cross-validated AUC it earned. That AUC is a leave-one-phylum-out estimate: the model is scored on a phylum it has never seen any member of, which is a harder test than a random split and is the appropriate one here, since the OTUs being predicted are mostly uncultured and sit outside the training distribution.

The dysbiosis score places a sample in a reference cohort. It is not a probability and the model's accuracy on a disease outside the thirteen in the cohort is lower than its accuracy inside it — AUC 0.64 against 0.80. Quote the first number.
